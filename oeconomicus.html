Oeconomicus

Oeconomicus is the first French journal entirely dedicated to the analysis and accessible dissemination of economic research. It brings together researchers, PhD students, students, and economists from both the private and public sectors, and provides a platform for the democratisation and debate of economic research findings and their practical applications.

Its philosophy starts from a simple observation: economics is present throughout daily life but remains poorly understood and too often confined to specialists. The journal seeks to combine scientific rigour with accessibility and to create a space in which economists and students can discuss, debate and share their work.
