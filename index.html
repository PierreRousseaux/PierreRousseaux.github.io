I am a PhD Candidate in Economics at CREST, supervised by Roland Rathelot and Arne Uhlendorff. My research studies how unemployment insurance shapes job-search behaviour and re-employment, the optimal design of the UI system, and how it may affect workers differently.
I visited the LSE Economics Department (STICERD) from February to July 2026, invited by Prof. Camille Landais.
I am affiliated with the Institute for Public Policies (IPP) and the Institute for Macroeconomic and International Policies (i-MIP).
