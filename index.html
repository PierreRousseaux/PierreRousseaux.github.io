I am a PhD candidate in Economics at CREST, supervised by Roland Rathelot and Arne Uhlendorff. My research focuses on labour economics and public economics, with particular emphasis on unemployment insurance, job-search behaviour and re-employment.

I visited the LSE Economics Department (STICERD) from February to July 2026, invited by Camille Landais. I am affiliated with the Institut des politiques publiques (IPP) and i-MIP.
